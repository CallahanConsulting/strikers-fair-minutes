Fair Minutes

Set your game format and roster. The planner splits every period at the water breaks and rotates players so minutes come out as even as possible.

Game setup

How to use Fair Minutes

Set up the game

  1. Team size: everyone available for this game.
  2. Players on field: how many play at once, including the keeper (7 for 7v7, 9 for 9v9).
  3. Periods: 2 for halves, 4 for quarters.
  4. Period length: minutes in each period.
  5. Water breaks per period: stops in the middle of each period where you can sub. Use 0 if you only sub between periods.
  6. Player names: one per line. Only the first names up to your team size are used.

Read the plan

  • Each period is split into equal shifts at the water breaks. You sub only at those stops.
  • The rotation grid shows who plays each shift and their total minutes. Totals in orange are one shift short.
  • Shift by shift cards list who's on and who sits, with game-clock times. Green names are coming on.

Tips

  • If minutes aren't exactly even, add a water break or change players on field. The page tells you when a split can't be even.
  • Shuffle start order changes who starts on the bench, so the same kids don't always sit first.
  • Absent player? Move them to the bottom of the list and lower team size.
  • Your roster and settings stay saved on this phone.
  • Add this page to your home screen to open it like an app at the field.
